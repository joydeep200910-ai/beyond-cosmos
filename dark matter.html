The Cosmic Mystery

The Invisible Universe

Everything we can directly see is only a small part of the universe. Two mysterious components dominate the cosmic picture: dark matter and dark energy.

01
INVISIBLE MATTER

Dark Matter

Dark matter does not appear to absorb, emit or reflect light in the way ordinary matter does. We infer its presence from its gravitational influence on galaxies, stars and light.

We can see its effects, not the matter itself.
02
COSMIC EXPANSION

Dark Energy

Dark energy is the name scientists give to the unknown phenomenon associated with the accelerating expansion of the universe.

Its true nature remains one of cosmology's greatest mysteries.

Two mysteries. One enormous universe.

Although dark matter and dark energy are both invisible, they are not the same thing. They appear to influence the universe in fundamentally different ways.

Following The Evidence

How Do We Know It's There?

Dark matter cannot be observed directly with ordinary telescopes. Instead, scientists study the gravitational effects it has on visible matter and light.

01
GR

Galaxy Rotation

Stars far from the centers of galaxies often orbit faster than expected from the amount of visible matter. This suggests that additional unseen mass contributes to the galaxy's gravity.

Gravity reveals what light cannot.
02
GL

Gravitational Lensing

Massive objects bend the path of light. By measuring this distortion, astronomers can map mass that may not be visible through ordinary observations.

Light traces the hidden mass.
03
GC

Galaxy Clusters

The motion of galaxies inside clusters and the gravitational effects observed around them indicate that much more mass is present than we can see.

Cosmic structures expose invisible gravity.
04
CS

Cosmic Structure

Dark matter plays an important role in models of how galaxies and large-scale structures formed throughout cosmic history.

The universe remembers its hidden architecture.
The Expanding Universe

The Universe Is Speeding Up.

The universe has been expanding since the Big Bang. But observations show that this expansion is accelerating. Scientists call the unknown cause of this acceleration dark energy.

COSMOS
01

Expansion

Distant galaxies are generally moving away from one another as space itself expands.

02

Acceleration

Observations indicate that the expansion of the universe is accelerating rather than slowing down.

03

The Mystery

We do not yet know what dark energy fundamentally is. Understanding it remains one of modern cosmology's greatest challenges.
